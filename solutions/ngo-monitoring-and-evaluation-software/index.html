06 · Common questions

Practical questions teams ask.

Use these answers to clarify scope, compare approaches and identify the decisions your team needs to make before implementation.

What operational problems can NGO monitoring and evaluation software address?

Monitoring and evaluation software should connect each indicator to a definition, source record, reporting period, responsible role and review decision. Its purpose is not to produce more charts; it is to help teams notice missing evidence and respond sooner.

What should a strong NGO monitoring and evaluation software approach include?

Start with indicator linked to source evidence. The minimum useful record set covers results framework and indicator definition, participant or unit of analysis, baseline, milestone and endline observations, data-quality checks and exceptions, reporting obligation and due date and analysis notes, limitations and approvals. Remove any field that does not support a service, safeguard, decision or reporting obligation.

What drives the cost and timeline of NGO monitoring and evaluation software?

The main cost and timeline drivers are workflow and permission complexity, data migration quality, integrations, offline or language needs, testing availability, training and long-term ownership. Estimate after modelling one real journey and its exceptions.

How should an NGO evaluate a partner for NGO monitoring and evaluation software?

Ask a prospective partner to model one real journey, one exception, one permission boundary and one report. Compare how they handle indicators without operational owners, changing definitions between reports, averages that hide attrition and AI-written narratives not checked against approved figures, not only the number of features in a demonstration.

When is a nonprofit ready to invest in NGO monitoring and evaluation software?

Readiness depends less on organisation size than on whether the team can name indicator linked to source evidence, the people who own each step, the decisions the system should support and the evidence needed to test a first release.
