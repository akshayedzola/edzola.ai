## Description:

## Solutions for Non-Profits:

- Create and run social media campaigns to promote your cause, increase awareness, and engage with your supporters. You can also schedule multiple posts at once for multiple social handles
- Respond quickly to your supporters and engage with them in a timely manner using Zoho Social's alerts feature, which sends notifications for all sorts of engagement, including mentions, comments, and direct messages.

- Measure the impact of your social media activities, identify areas for improvement, and make data-driven decisions using inbuilt analytics and reporting
