## Description:

Perform offline field data collection with Forms. When online, embed them on your website to get volunteers and donations for your non-profit or send them out to collect data from your beneficiaries.

## Solutions for Non-Profits:

- Create customized forms with drag and drop fields for multiple purposes like event registrations, volunteer sign-ups, feedback collection etc.,
- Track the source of your data and identify what performs better - such as social media or email campaigns, and optimize your fundraising efforts accordingly
- Customize format (standard/card) to improve user experience, configure rules/logic, translate it to suit your beneficiries comfort language
- Use the offline data collection feature to collect information in areas with limited internet connectivity, such as remote communities or rural areas.
- Set-up approval processes, ensure security with double opt-in and OTP verification
