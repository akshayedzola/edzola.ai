## Description:

Manage your staff's and volunteers' information, attendance, performance, and more, from a centralized platform. With its customizable features, robust analytics, and self-service capabilities, your nonprofit can streamline their HR processes, increase employee engagement, and improve organizational effectiveness.

## Solutions for Non-Profits:

- Train staff & volunteers on topics related to your mission, such as fundraising, advocacy, or program delivery. Create courses, quizzes, and assessments to track the progress of your learners and ensure they have the skills and knowledge to support the organization's goals.
- Track the hours worked by your staff and volunteers, manage the time off, including vacation, sick days, and other types of leave and ensure they are paid accurately.
- Use the case management feature to track the requests and inquiries they receive from your stakeholders, such as donors, clients, or community members and assign cases to specific staff members
- Schedule shifts for staff to manage workload better. Provide self-service portals for volunteers where they can update their progress
