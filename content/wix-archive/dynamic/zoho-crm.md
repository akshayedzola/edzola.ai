## Description:

A customizable and integrated donor management platform that makes every mission possible for non-profits of all types and sizes. Set up a central database, drive lasting impact with omnichannel messaging, use smart webforms to improve engagement, and make communications with your stakeholders easy to manage.

## Solutions for Non-Profits:

- Manage all your stakeholders (donors, partners, beneficiaries etc.,) data in one place and give them access to their data where they can update details using self-service portals
- Contact your stakeholders in ways they prefer - call them, send an email, use live chat, set up meetings - from one place
- Configure the view for the users from different programs and choose what data can be accessed by them.
- Effectively track grants, scholarships and donor contributions with the pipeline management system.
