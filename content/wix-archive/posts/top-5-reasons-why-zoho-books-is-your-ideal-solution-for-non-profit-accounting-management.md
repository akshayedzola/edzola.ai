Non-profit organizations often encounter distinctive challenges in their accounting practices. Non-profit organizations face complex accounting challenges, such as **tracking restricted funds, managing grants and donations, and complying with strict reporting requirements.** Furthermore, these organizations need to ensure accurate allocations of funds across various programs and projects. **With Zoho Books, non-profits can streamline their accounting processes, ensuring transparency, accuracy, and compliance.**
