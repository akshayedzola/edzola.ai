There are two things that make the place a better place to live. One is when someone takes up an existing problem or complexity and brings a solution to the table. Another is when one turns a creative idea into reality even if it's not going to solve any critical problem but rather provide a luxury. This start-up is a result of the former. Our founder, Akshay who worked as a software engineer at Zoho, came to witness a social challenge during his fellowship at [Teach For India](https://www.teachforindia.org/). This realization didn't stop but made way to develop a thoughtful, social organization called EdZola.

EdZola was founded in 2020 and since then started to work on providing solutions to existing social problems with the use of technology.

There is still a lot to know about beginning such an organization and getting people to solve real world problems. To understand these better, the founder himself has given a podcast communicating the why’s and what’s of the company. This will make the picture complete by understanding some of - why EdZola , what sparked it, how it became a part of social organization, what it is trying to do and most importantly how it impacts the underprivileged.
