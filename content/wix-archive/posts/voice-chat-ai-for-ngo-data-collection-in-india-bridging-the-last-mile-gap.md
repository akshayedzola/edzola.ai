## **Why last-mile data is hard and why voice changes the game**

Across health, livelihoods, education, and climate programs, NGOs struggle with **low literacy, app fatigue, limited devices and patchy connectivity** all of which lead to partial, delayed or biased data. India’s **“AI for social impact”** momentum is real, spanning healthcare, education, agriculture and the environment, but last-mile capture remains the bottleneck.

Two India-scale examples show the promise of inclusive AI: **flood forecasting** that now serves 80+ countries and **sector-specific AI from Wadhwani AI** for farmers and maternal/child health proof that AI can deliver at scale when designed for constraints.

## **Voice vs. legacy IVR vs. chatbots: what actually works in 2025**

**Legacy IVR** helped for basic hotlines, but it struggles with free-form inputs, multilingual nuance and drop-offs. **Conversational Voice AI and chat** let beneficiaries and field teams **speak naturally** (or type) in their language, capture richer context and hand off to humans when needed. India’s national stack is also moving fast on **multilingual AI** (Bhashini), reinforcing why **language inclusion** matters for public services and social programs.

Meanwhile, established survey tools such as **KoBoToolbox** prove how critical **offline data collection** is for field realities store-and-forward, retry logic and resilient uploads. That same offline-first philosophy is now expected of voice/chat AI too.

## **Five High-impact NGO use cases**

### How do frontline health workers use voice calls for follow-ups? *( SDG 3 – Good Health & Well-Being )*

For NGOs in maternal and child health (MCH), community medicine, or last-mile public health, Zolabs  enables beneficiary engagement without smartphones or apps. Frontline workers simply call in local languages to log treatment follow-ups, send immunization reminders, and capture symptom updates. Through this, they can track program impact more effectively.

### **How can teachers and M&E teams track student learning and training outcomes by call?** ***( SDG 4 – Quality Education )***

[**Zolabs.ai**](http://Zolabs.ai) lets teachers and M&E staff gather everything by voice, student attendance, learning performance and activity outcomes over a quick call or chat. The same flow captures **teacher-training reflections and session feedback**, so outcomes are logged without apps or forms. Behind the scenes, ZoLabs instantly **transcribes, structures and summarizes** what’s spoken, turning conversations into **dashboard-ready reports** for program reviews and funder updates.

### How can NGOs track livelihoods & enable inclusion for persons with disabilities? *( SDG 8 – Decent Work | SDG 10 – Reduced Inequalities )*

Let voice-based check-ins to capture employment status, wages, workplace safety and skilling feedback all via short phone calls in local languages, no digital literacy needed. It’s also a perfect fit for disability inclusion programs, helping visually impaired or low-literacy individuals share job updates or support needs. All inputs are transcribed, tagged and structured to power dashboards and impact reports.

### How to set up disaster intake & environmental incident logs by voice? *( SDG 13/14/15 — Climate Action / Life Below Water / Life on Land )*

Stand up hotlines for floods/heatwaves and log forest/wildlife incidents with geo + priority scoring. Run rapid loss-and-damage voice surveys; [ZoLabs.ai](http://ZoLabs.ai) converts conversations into actionable queues and weekly exports for relief planning and donor evidence.

### Best way to conduct poverty & welfare surveys in low-tech areas? *( SDG 1 – No Poverty )*

Frontline workers collect crucial data on household income, social protection gaps, and livelihood challenges directly by phone. Every voice call is auto-tagged, categorized, and synced into your MIS, enabling smarter targeting for cash transfers, rations or housing support.
