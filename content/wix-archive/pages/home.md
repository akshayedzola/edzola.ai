# Custom MIS, CRM & AI Solutions for Nonprofits That Work at Scale

Trusted by 100+ organisations across India, US, and the UK, EdZola builds custom Management Information Systems (MIS), deploys CRM, and develops AI tools that turn messy data into real-time decisions and drive impact at scale.

## What we do

Systems of Record  AI AccelerationBuilt for Impact Program

### Every Nonprofit Needs a Foundation

EdZola builds the Systems of Record nonprofits run on-Program MIS, CRM, HRMS, and fundraising systems. Designed, configured, migrated, and maintained through ImpactOS, our proven delivery methodology for nonprofit data systems. We work across education, livelihoods, health, and skilling programmes.

Note: A System of Record (SOR) is a single source of truth where your organization’s critical data-people, programs, and outcomes-is securely stored and managed.

## How do we operate?

![consult with EdZola](../media/946993_14d2c08f4a904f0285507cabf0c57017_mv2.webp)

### Diagnose (1–2 weeks)

We map your programme logic, interview end-users, audit existing data tools, and produce a System Blueprint - before writing a single line of code. Every project starts with a free discovery call.

![Design, Develop and Build](../media/946993_4e82dbc9d2504c86bfa558c67a66dc76_mv2.webp)

### Build in Agile Sprints (6–16 weeks)

We develop in 2–4 week sprint cycles on Zoho Creator, Zoho CRM, or custom platforms. You review working outputs after every sprint - no big-bang reveals. Typical MIS builds go live within 3–4 months.

![Get Support for your Nonprofits with EdZola](../media/946993_ff34123707d643f79cbb8a1ce1071d25_mv2.webp)

### Launch & Evolve (30–60 day warranty + ongoing)

We train your team, migrate your data, and stay on for 30-60 days post go-live. Then we keep evolving the system as your programmes grow.
