# The Story Behind It!

Today, thanks to various tech enthusiasts within NGO's & Tech4Good providers, a lot of NGO's scale EXPONENTIALLY with technology and the modern NGO sector's issue lies in bringing all learnings and experience out in the open from the silos they currently in!

​

That's when the idea of SIC was born!

​

When we thought a bit deeper, these emerged as the pressing issues that could be solved by a platform that can bring those like-minded people together

![EdZola | Innovate Idea](../media/946993_e7460659f43d4e799763c958204f1879_mv2.webp)

## Inefficiency in reaching non-profits

Despite the development of various products to aid the social sector, how much of this innovation is truly reaching the non-profits?

![EdZola | Visibility in the Social Sector](../media/946993_ffdb93293a4c4578818b987c39e18214_mv2.webp)

# 2

## Lack of visibility

There's a substantial effort in creating tech solutions, but how much of it is visible to fellow nonprofit professionals, causing unnecessary duplication of work?

We aimed to bring together the tech-forward nonprofits and Tech4Good enthusiasts who strive to create impact through collaboration, sharing ideas, and learning from each other.

With this aim in mind, we set out to create a platform where all these happen:

Share failures and redundant efforts, helping other organizations to save costs and avoid the same pitfalls.

### Knowledge Sharing

A shared platform that multiple like-minded organizations can leverage, enhancing unity and resource-sharing.

### Collaborative platform building

Identify and utilize existing pre-built solutions, accelerating progress and minimizing wasted effort.

## Diverse Initiatives & Sessions

### 1. Tech4Good Partners

The more the better! We brought complementary Tech4Good vendors together so the NGOs can get solutions from experts across the spectrum in one place!

![EdZola Blogs](../media/946993_c53a57d23c234ff5a674fba4ce26797e_mv2.webp)![EdZola | Connect | Learn | Inspire](../media/946993_821c4de2516e47f9bb6629c8951b7447_mv2.webp)

### 2. Capacity Building Workshops

Experts from the social sector who have years of experience conduct a resource-filled workshops on the burning problems of NGOs

### 3. Lightning Talks

Leaders from various Tech4Good organizations share their insights, experiences and learnings in a power packed 7 minute lightning sessions.

![EdZola | Social Impact Circle - insightful Conversation](../media/946993_abf1ba2133444f8a9a322118119c8b14_mv2.webp)![Impact-driven organization leaders discussed modern challenges and tech solutions for NGO's.](../media/946993_a23776898dad4dd3bc7a902f58574c6b_mv2.webp)

### 4. Panel Discussions

Impact-driven organization leaders discussed modern challenges and tech solutions for NGO's. This exchange of ideas marks a significant step towards effective and sustainable change.

### 5. Interaction in Circles

Our core session of the event 'Social Impact Circle' where Nonprofits are encouraged to share their learnings, ideas and plans for technology in multiple groups with a Tech4Good leader for each group.

![Our core session of the event 'Social Impact Circle' where Nonprofits are encouraged to share their learnings, ideas and plan](../media/946993_0fd48622ca8c4e54b108398e3aa97667_mv2.webp)![EdZola | Data collected in the Social Impact Circle](../media/946993_9221d5f3f8f7457987892e6fb22b87cc_mv2.webp)

### 6. Showcase

The valuable pointers that come up in the circle activity shouldn't stay in silos. So we showcase them for every participant to see and learn

### 7. Networking

An open space for everyone to interact and connect on a deeper level to put all the discussed ideas into action!
