## The relationship gaps holding you back

Nonprofits survive on relationships — with donors, volunteers, members, and partners. Without a system, those relationships fall through the cracks.

📧

#### Donor follow-ups get missed

A potential funder expressed interest three months ago. Who followed up? When? Nobody knows — because it's in someone's inbox, not a system.

🗂️

#### Every team runs its own tools

Fundraising uses Google Sheets. HR uses email. Finance uses Tally. Nothing talks to anything. Data lives in silos that nobody can cross.

⏳

#### Onboarding takes forever

New hires wait days for documentation. Leave policies live in PDFs. Expense claims are manual. Your team spends time on admin instead of impact.

📊

#### You can't report on what you can't see

How many donors gave last year? What's your retention rate? How much did you spend on travel? Without connected systems, every answer requires a research project.

The 3Es Framework

How we think about every CRM implementation.

A CRM is not Excel with lipstick. It's a living system that makes relationships come alive. Every project we take on is anchored in three principles.

E1

#### Efficiency

Save time. Reduce errors. Streamline workflows. If your team is doing something manually that a system should handle, we fix that first. We turn 10-step processes into 3.

E2

#### Experience

Improve the quality of every stakeholder interaction. If a donor once felt ignored, they should now feel remembered. If a volunteer felt stuck, they should now feel valued. We test for delight, not just functionality.

E3

#### Empowerment

Help your team move from "Excel comfort zone" to data-driven confidence. Even if a project stops after Phase 1, you should walk away with something valuable — process clarity, optimised flows, a roadmap.
