## Privacy Policy

EdZola is committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal data responsibly in accordance with applicable laws, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India).

Information We Collect

We may collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, and organization details through forms on our website.

Purpose of Data Collection

Your data is collected and used only for specific purposes, including responding to inquiries, providing services, and improving our offerings.

Consent

By submitting your information, you provide consent for EdZola to process your personal data for the stated purposes. You may withdraw consent at any time by contacting us.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. Information may be shared with trusted platforms (such as Zoho) strictly for service delivery and operational purposes.

Data Security

We implement reasonable security safeguards to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure.

Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected or as required by law.

Your Rights (DPDP)

As a data principal under the DPDP Act, you have the right to:

- Access your personal data
- Request correction or erasure
- Withdraw consent
- Raise grievances regarding data usage

Grievance Redressal

For any concerns related to your data, you can contact us at [reachus@edzola.com](mailto:reachus@edzola.com). We will address your request within a reasonable timeframe.

Cookies

We may use cookies to enhance user experience and analyze website traffic.

Updates to Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the website indicates your acceptance of these updates.

Contact

For privacy-related queries, reach us at [reachus@edzola.com](mailto:reachus@edzola.com?subject=Data%20Privacy%20Inquiry)
