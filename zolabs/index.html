The gap you already feel

Between having nothing and doing a formal evaluation — there's Zolabs.

You have dashboards. You have reports. What you don't have is an honest answer to the harder question: did anything actually shift for the person on the other side?

Forms fail at last mile

A significant part of the communities you work with don't have a stable smartphone, consistent internet, or the comfort to type long answers on a small screen. They do have access to a basic phone — and they know how to pick up a call.

Checklists can't capture agency

"How many attended?" is an important question. But it tells you nothing about whether the intervention built confidence, changed behaviour, or left someone feeling more heard — or less. That nuance lives in conversation, not in a tick-box.

The analysis layer is a black box

Most "AI for social impact" tools collect narratives and then run a magic prompt. The analytical rigour isn't visible, isn't explainable, and can't be trusted when your donor asks hard questions.

Data arrives late and disconnected

By the time field responses are cleaned, translated, and entered, the moment for course-correction has passed. Your frontline workers, your beneficiaries, and your dashboards are all speaking different languages.

The Zolabs model

Listen at scale. Without losing the human texture.

01

Design your listening

Describe what you need to understand — not just what you need to count. Zolabs helps you build a form that blends structured fields with open narrative questions. You even note why you're collecting each field. That shifts the mindset from "collect everything" to "collect only what truly matters."

02

AI calls your people

Natural, multilingual conversations — not IVR menus, not WhatsApp bots. Zolabs reaches beneficiaries on any phone, in 25+ Indian and international languages, with dynamic branching that follows the person rather than the script. A hesitant pause on a call tells you something a ticked box never could.

03

Structure emerges from conversation

Every call produces a structured insight alongside a full transcript and audio recording. Quantitative fields and qualitative narratives, together — synced automatically into your CRM, MIS, or data warehouse. You own the raw data. Always.
