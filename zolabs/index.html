The gap you already feel

Between a form and a formal evaluation, too much experience goes unheard.

Forms and surveys are useful, but they lose context. Traditional qualitative research preserves nuance, but is hard to repeat at programme scale. Zolabs sits in that gap.

01 / REACH

Forms fail at the last mile.

Literacy, device access and survey fatigue decide whose experience makes it into the dataset.

02 / DEPTH

Checklists cannot capture agency.

A rating says what changed. A conversation helps explain how, why and for whom.

03 / TRUST

Analysis can become a black box.

A polished theme means little when a reviewer cannot trace it to the words that support it.

04 / TIME

Insight arrives after the decision.

Transcription and coding backlogs turn living programme feedback into a late report.

The Zolabs model

From a research question to evidence your team can inspect.

The workflow keeps collection, interpretation and review connected instead of treating the call as the end of the work.

01 / FRAME

Start with what you need to learn.

Define the evaluation question, audience, language and boundaries for the conversation.

02 / LISTEN

Let the AI run the phone interviews.

Reach participants at scale while allowing an answer to unfold in their own words.

03 / UNDERSTAND

Move from transcript to reviewed finding.

Organise themes and structured responses, then let a researcher verify the evidence behind them.

Evidence, not just output

Every conclusion should have a way back to the conversation.

The goal is not to replace a researcher’s judgement. It’s to take away the admin around listening, so teams can spend more time asking better questions and interpreting what they hear.

  • Keep source material alongside structured findings
  • Review AI-generated themes before they become claims
  • Combine what people said with programme data
  • Carry exceptions and contradictions into the final story

Where it fits

Every moment where a real conversation matters.

Use Zolabs when the programme needs more than a checkbox but cannot send an interviewer everywhere.

Evaluation

Impact and outcome studies

Collect mixed-method evidence across a large participant group while preserving explanations and lived experience.

Programme learning

Journey-based follow-ups

Check in with learners, patients, mothers, alumni or entrepreneurs at the moments that matter.

Accountability

Research

Tracking

Operations

Onboarding and verification

Where Zolabs is going

From voice evaluation to an end-to-end evidence workspace.

Today the product is centred on evaluation through AI phone interviews. Next is a broader platform that supports a study from start to finish without erasing researcher judgement.

Today
Voice-led evaluation

AI interviews in local languages, managing participants and calls, transcripts, pulling out answers, and review.

Expanding
Deeper analysis

Stronger coding, comparison across groups and clearer links between programme data and qualitative evidence.

Direction
Study-to-report workflow

Research design, evidence management, collaborative interpretation and reporting in one system where every finding can be traced.
