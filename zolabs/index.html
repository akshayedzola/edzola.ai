The gap you already feel

Between a form and a formal evaluation, too much experience goes unheard.

Structured tools are useful, but they flatten context. Traditional qualitative research preserves nuance, but is hard to repeat at programme scale. Zolabs sits in that gap.

01 / REACH

Forms fail at the last mile.

Literacy, device access and survey fatigue decide whose experience makes it into the dataset.

02 / DEPTH

Checklists cannot capture agency.

A rating says what changed. A conversation helps explain how, why and for whom.

03 / TRUST

Analysis can become a black box.

A polished theme means little when a reviewer cannot trace it to the words that support it.

04 / TIME

Insight arrives after the decision.

Transcription and coding backlogs turn living programme feedback into a late report.

The Zolabs model

From a research question to evidence your team can inspect.

The workflow keeps collection, interpretation and review connected instead of treating the call as the end of the work.

01 / FRAME

Start with what you need to learn.

Define the evaluation question, audience, language and boundaries for the conversation.

02 / LISTEN

Let voice AI conduct the interviews.

Reach participants at scale while allowing an answer to unfold in their own words.

03 / UNDERSTAND

Move from transcript to reviewed finding.

Organise themes and structured responses, then let a researcher verify the evidence behind them.

Evidence, not just output

Every conclusion should have a way back to the conversation.

The goal is not to replace a researcher’s judgement. It is to remove the operational drag around listening, so teams can spend more time asking better questions and interpreting what they hear.

  • Keep source material alongside structured findings
  • Review AI-generated themes before they become claims
  • Combine qualitative voice with programme data
  • Carry exceptions and contradictions into the final story

Where Zolabs is going

From voice evaluation to an end-to-end evidence workspace.
