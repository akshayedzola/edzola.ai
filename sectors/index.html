How we build it · the MAP approach

The same three steps, in the same order, for every sector.

Fix the data first. AI comes last, and it's better for it.

M

Model

Start with the decisions your team needs to make. Every field earns its place, and the records connect through one ID.

A

Align

Fit the system to how your field team already works: offline, in the local language, with a screen for each role and follow-ups that fire on their own.

P

Power with AI

On clean records, add AI where it saves real hours. A person checks every output, and some things stay off-limits.
