How we build · MAP after the playbook

Different programme models. The same discipline for building the right system.

First identify how your organisation creates change. Then model the work, align the system to real practice, and add AI only where it earns its keep.

M

Model the work

Start with your operating model and the decisions your team makes. Build the records and relationships around them, not around a generic sector template.

A

Align

Fit the system to how your field team already works: offline, in the local language, with a screen for each role and follow-ups that fire on their own.

P

Power with AI

On clean records, add AI where it saves real hours. A person checks every output, and some things stay off-limits.

How we work with you

From one tracker to a system your team actually runs.

Most systems launch in three to four months. The timeline moves with the number of programmes and roles, how messy the old data is, and how many tools need connecting.

Diagnosis

1 to 2 weeks

We map how your programme works, talk to the people who will use it, review the tools you use today and hand you a System Blueprint, a clear plan of what we'll build.

Build in stages

6 to 16 weeks

Working screens every 2 to 4 weeks, tested by your field team in your own terms. We clean up your old spreadsheets and move them across.

Launch, then stay

30 to 60 days

Field training and launch, then close support until your team can run it confidently.
