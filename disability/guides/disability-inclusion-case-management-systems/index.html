06 · Common questions

Practical questions teams ask.

Use these answers to clarify scope, compare approaches and identify the decisions your team needs to make before implementation.

How should a nonprofit implement disability inclusion case management systems?

Use a phased sequence: co-design language and access needs, record goals before service categories, map barriers and accommodations, assign and follow referrals, review whether participation changed and give people a correction route. Each stage needs a named owner and a review gate before the next stage begins.

What records and data fields are needed for disability inclusion case management systems?

Start with person-centred inclusion plan. The minimum useful record set covers person, communication preference and consent, goal and participation context, functional need and environmental barrier, accommodation or assistive support, referral, service and follow-up and participation outcome and person feedback. Remove any field that does not support a service, safeguard, decision or reporting obligation.

Which staff roles and review steps should disability inclusion case management systems include?

Give every stage a named operational owner and require a separate human review for clinical diagnosis, eligibility and benefit decisions, accepting an accommodation as adequate and sharing sensitive functional information. Use requested accommodations delivered, service use confirmed, participation goals progressed and records corrected by the person to decide whether the workflow is ready to expand.

What are the most common mistakes when setting up disability inclusion case management systems?

The main risks are diagnosis used as identity, collecting impairment detail without purpose, accommodation marked complete before use and inaccessible forms and communication. Test these with representative cases and keep the consequential judgement with a named person.

How should disability inclusion case management systems change for a small NGO versus a multi-state programme?

Keep the definition of person-centred inclusion plan stable at every scale. A small programme can begin with one workflow and a few roles; a multi-state programme also needs delegated ownership, stronger permissions, shared definitions, integration rules and location-level quality review.
