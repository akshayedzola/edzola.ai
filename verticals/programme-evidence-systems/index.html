Programme & evidence systems

Know who received what, what changed, and what needs attention.

We design programme MIS, case management, monitoring, evaluation and reporting systems around the journey your participants, partners and field teams actually take. One useful record from first contact to outcome, with evidence that can travel into decisions and reports.

What breaks today

The report is only as reliable as the journey underneath it.

Most reporting problems begin earlier: duplicate identities, unclear stages, offline delays, changing indicators or follow-ups that live outside the system.

01 · FRAGMENTED JOURNEYS

The same person appears in several spreadsheets.

Teams cannot see a complete history of enrolment, services, assessments, referrals and outcomes.

02 · LATE VISIBILITY

Managers discover drop-offs after the programme ends.

Operational warning signs stay buried until a monthly or quarterly reporting cycle.

03 · REPORTING PRESSURE

Every funder asks for a different version of the truth.

Programme and M&E teams rebuild numbers manually because indicators and source records are not connected.

End-to-end workflow

From programme design to the next decision.

The system should support daily delivery and organisational learning. If it only produces a dashboard, the hard part is still happening elsewhere.

  1. Design, outcomes and targets
  2. Enrolment, consent and identity
  3. Services, attendance and cases
  4. Assessments, referrals and milestones
  5. Follow-up, outcomes and data quality
  6. Decisions, learning and reports
Programme MIS & case management

A working system for the frontline, not only a reporting database.

We start with the operating model, roles and decisions. The technology follows: often Zoho Creator or CRM, sometimes custom applications, and always the smallest stack that can be supported.

Participants, households and organisations

Stable identities, consent, relationships, programme eligibility, locations and history across multiple interventions.

Programmes, cohorts and services

Programme structure, partner delivery, batches, sessions, attendance, entitlements and field activities.

Case work and referrals

Needs, risk flags, action plans, notes, documents, referrals, follow-ups and role-based visibility.

Field and partner experience

Mobile-friendly forms, offline needs, partner portals, validations, task queues and supervisor review.

Monitoring, evaluation & reporting

Evidence should change a decision, not just fill a dashboard.

We connect indicators to source records, owners, review cadences and reporting obligations so teams can see both what happened and where they need to act.

01

Results framework

Translate Theory of Change, logframe and funder commitments into indicators the system can actually produce.

02

Data quality and accountability

Build validations, exception queues, review roles, audit history and definitions into the workflow.

03

Decision cadence

Design dashboards and reports around the weekly, monthly and quarterly conversations they must support.

04

Responsible assistance

Use AI for transcription, classification, summarisation or anomaly review only with traceable evidence and a named human decision-maker.

Part of organisation-wide transformation

Programme data becomes more useful when funding, people and finance can meet it.

A programme system can be the first release in a wider roadmap. We stabilise the participant journey first, then connect donor reports, grant obligations, staff allocation, expenses and leadership decisions in sequence.
