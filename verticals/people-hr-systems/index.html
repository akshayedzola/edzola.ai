People & HR systems

Give every employee a reliable journey from hiring to exit.

We design HRMS and people operations systems for distributed, mission-led teams. Bring recruitment, employee records, onboarding, leave, attendance, payroll inputs, performance, expenses and offboarding into one clear lifecycle.

What breaks today

People operations become invisible work.

HR teams spend their time chasing forms, updating duplicate records and reconciling attendance or payroll inputs instead of supporting people and managers.

01 · NO SHARED RECORD

Employee information is scattered.

Contracts, roles, managers, locations, documents and changes live across files, inboxes and payroll sheets.

02 · MANUAL CYCLES

Every month restarts the chase.

Attendance, leave, expenses and payroll inputs depend on reminders and manual consolidation.

03 · WEAK EXPERIENCE

Employees cannot answer simple questions themselves.

Requests, policies, balances, goals and documents require an HR team member to look them up.

Employee lifecycle

One journey, with clean ownership at every stage.

Not every organisation needs every module on day one. The sequence should follow the workload and risk your HR team carries now.

  1. Workforce plan and recruitment
  2. Offer, joining and onboarding
  3. Employee records and self-service
  4. Leave, attendance and expenses
  5. Performance, growth and payroll inputs
  6. Transition, exit and alumni record
Core HRMS

Start with the employee record, then earn the complexity.

We configure the system around real policy, approval and payroll boundaries rather than copying a generic enterprise setup.

Recruitment and joining

Roles, candidates, interviews, decisions, offers, document collection, checklists and accountable onboarding owners.

Employee master and self-service

Organisation structure, employment history, documents, policies, requests, letters and the fields employees can safely maintain.

Time, leave and expenses

Attendance patterns, holidays, leave policy, approvals, travel or expense requests and exception handling.

Performance and growth

Goals, check-ins, review cycles, feedback, learning, role changes and manager visibility without turning people into scores.

Payroll and statutory inputs

Validated monthly inputs, changes, deductions, reimbursements and handoffs to the organisation’s payroll and finance process.

Exit and institutional memory

Clearance, asset return, access removal, knowledge handover, final documents and an auditable employment history.

The handoffs that matter

HR does not operate on an island.

People data touches programme delivery, project staffing, grant allocations, expenses, finance, access control and organisational learning. We connect only the records each team legitimately needs.

01

HR ↔ finance

Payroll inputs, reimbursements, cost centres and approved changes, with a clear source of truth.

02

HR ↔ grants and projects

Staff allocation and role information where budgets or delivery plans require it, without exposing private HR data.

03

HR ↔ access and security

Joining, role changes and exits trigger the right account, device and permission actions.

04

Employee ↔ manager

Self-service for routine needs and timely prompts for the decisions only a manager can make.

Part of organisation-wide transformation

People systems can stabilise the organisation while wider change unfolds.

HRMS is often a practical early release: it creates clean roles, teams, reporting lines and access patterns that later support projects, finance, grants and programme systems. We stage the change so daily people operations keep running.
