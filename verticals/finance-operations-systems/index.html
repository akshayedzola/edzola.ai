Finance & operations systems

Make budgets, approvals, projects and delivery move together.

We design the operational layer around accounting: budget control, expense workflows, fund utilisation, project delivery, task ownership, documents and approvals. Finance keeps the books; connected operations give every team the right view before month-end.

What breaks today

The plan, the work and the spend drift apart.

Accounting software can be accurate while project owners still cannot see budget pressure, approval status or what evidence finance needs from them.

01 · APPROVAL FOG

Requests move through chat and email.

No one knows what is waiting, who owns the decision or which document is missing.

02 · LATE ACTUALS

Budget visibility arrives after decisions are made.

Project and grant owners manage commitments in parallel sheets because actuals are hard to interpret.

03 · SCATTERED WORK

Projects have tasks, but not one operating rhythm.

Owners, dependencies, risks, documents and time live across disconnected tools.

Plan-to-evidence flow

Connect the work before automating it.

The operational system should preserve accounting boundaries while giving teams timely, role-appropriate information.

  1. Plan, budget and ownership
  2. Request and approval
  3. Project, task and commitment
  4. Expense, invoice and payment handoff
  5. Actuals, allocation and variance
  6. Evidence, review and reforecast
Finance & expense management

Give programme and project owners a useful view without rebuilding accounting.

We design requests, approvals, allocations and management views around the accounting source of truth and the organisation’s control process.

Budgets and revisions

Programme, project, grant, cost centre and budget-head structures with approved changes and version history.

Requests and approvals

Purchase, travel, advance, expense and payment workflows with thresholds, evidence and accountable decisions.

Allocation and utilisation

Map commitments and actuals to the correct programme, grant or project and surface variance early.

Accounting handoff

Validated records, reconciliation status and integration boundaries for tools such as Zoho Books or Tally.

The accounting ledger and statutory treatment remain with the organisation’s authorised finance process. We design the operational records and controls that feed it reliably.

Project & work management

Make ownership and dependencies visible before another meeting is added.

Project systems are useful when they reflect how decisions, documents, risks, time and money move—not when they become a second to-do list.

01

Portfolio and project structure

Projects, milestones, workstreams, owners, dates, dependencies and shared definitions of done.

02

Work and review

Tasks, recurring routines, approvals, risks, decisions and escalation with a clear operating cadence.

03

Time, documents and costs

Timesheets where useful, document control, planned effort, commitments and the finance handoff.

04

Leadership view

Progress, blocked work, capacity, risk and budget signals that trace back to owned actions.

Marketing & outreach

Connect communication to the relationship it is meant to move.

Campaigns, journeys and events can sit inside this operating layer when they need to feed a donor, participant, member or partner record. We design segmentation, consent, ownership and response handoffs before choosing campaign tooling.

Audiences and consent

Segments, interests, communication preferences and lawful, organisation-approved consent handling.

Campaigns and journeys

Messages, events, automations, responses and conversion into a real relationship or programme workflow.

Part of organisation-wide transformation

Operations is where separate systems either connect or create more work.

We often sequence this pillar after core programme, funding or HR records are stable. Then approvals, projects, expenses and reporting can use trusted data instead of multiplying reconciliation work.
