How it works

From programme description
to full blueprint — in one session.

No workshops. No long discovery cycles. Just a conversation with ImpactOS — and a complete, ready-to-build MIS design at the end.

1

Describe your programme

Tell ImpactOS about your organisation, what you do, who you serve, and what data you need to track. Plain language is fine — no tech jargon required.

2

AI generates your blueprint

ImpactOS maps your Theory of Change, designs your data model, specifies your modules, plans your dashboards, and builds your sprint plan — in minutes.
