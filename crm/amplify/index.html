Why a cohort

Learn in a pod of 4 to 5 CRM users

Zoho's documentation tells you how to connect. It doesn't tell you which prompt works on a messy donor list, or what broke for the team next door. You learn that from people with the same tool and the same problems.

  • Shared prompts. What works in one CRM usually works in yours. Pods swap prompts every week.
  • Shared mistakes. Each pod shows one thing that worked and one that broke, so you skip the dead ends.
  • A community after. Three months in the alumni group, a monthly show-and-tell and a growing prompt library.

Safe by design

Four rules we teach on day one

Your CRM holds donor data. Every workflow in the cohort follows these rules.

Your own login

Claude sees only what you can see. Turn on only the tools a task needs.

Read first

Prompts start read-only. A person approves anything that changes a record.

Drafts, not sends

Emails to donors are saved as drafts for a person to review.

Guard your links

Connector URLs work like passwords. Don't share them, and disconnect what you stop using.

Questions

Before you sign up

Can just one person from our organisation join?

Yes. One person with a paid Zoho CRM or Bigin licence is enough. The price is the same for one or two people.

Why do I need a paid Claude plan?

Connecting Claude to Zoho uses a custom connector, which is only on paid Claude plans. Each participant connects with their own Claude account.

Do I have to share my screen or our data?

No. You build on your own CRM, on your own screen. Sharing in sessions is optional. If you want somewhere safe to practise, add a few test records to your own account. Our team never connects to your system.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Everything happens in Claude and inside Zoho's own screens. If you can build a report in your CRM, you can do this.

We use CRM for programmes, not fundraising. Is it still useful?

Yes. The examples lean on fundraising because that's where most teams start, but the same methods work for partnerships, volunteers or beneficiary follow-ups.

Does Zia Agents in Bigin work on every edition?

Zia Agents need Bigin Premier or Bigin 360. On other editions you still build a Claude agent in week 4.
