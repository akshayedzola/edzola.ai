A community of practice

You don't need another course. You need peers.

Everything you need to connect Claude to Zoho is already online. Most teams still don't do it, because learning alone is slow and there's nobody to ask when it doesn't work. Amplify puts you in a small group of nonprofits facing the same problems, with EdZola alongside.

  • Learn in pods of 4 to 5 nonprofits. A fundraising lead, a founder and a CRM admin from different organisations, working through the same problems.
  • Share what works. Every request that helps one team goes into a shared library for everyone.
  • Keep going after the four weeks. A monthly meet-up, a community group, and what's new in Zoho and Claude explained in plain language.

Our sector doesn't need more CRM developers. It needs CRM amplifiers: people inside nonprofits who help their teams get far more from the systems they already have.

Questions

Before you sign up

Is this a course or a community?

Both, in that order. The four weeks are structured, with a session and a task each week. After that, you stay in a community of nonprofits doing the same work, with a monthly meet-up and a shared library.

Can just one person from our organisation join?

Yes. One person with a paid Zoho CRM or Bigin licence is enough. The price is the same for one or two people.

Why do I need a paid Claude plan?

Connecting Claude to Zoho is only possible on paid Claude plans. Each participant connects with their own Claude account.

Do I have to share my screen or our data?

No. You build on your own CRM, on your own screen. Sharing in sessions is optional. If you want somewhere safe to practise, add a few test records to your own account. Our team never connects to your system.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Everything happens in Claude and inside Zoho's own screens. If you can build a report in your CRM, you can do this.

We use CRM for programmes, not fundraising. Is it still useful?

Yes. The examples lean on fundraising because that's where most teams start, but the same methods work for partnerships, volunteers or beneficiary follow-ups.

Zoho has its own AI, Zia. Do we still need Claude?

They do different things. We show both. On Bigin Premier and Bigin 360 you can also try Zia Agents in week 4, and compare them with what Claude does on the same job.
