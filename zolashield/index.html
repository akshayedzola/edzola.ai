The problem

DPDP compliance starts with knowing what data you hold. Most organisations don't.

Before consent, retention policies, or rights management can happen — you need a data inventory. Building one manually is where most compliance programmes quietly stall.

Personal data lives in places no one mapped

HR systems, field tools, WhatsApp groups, Google Sheets, and partner portals all hold personal data. IT audits catch the obvious systems. The rest — where the real risk lives — stays invisible.

Staff won't fill a 40-column spreadsheet

Manual data discovery templates get completion rates below 20%. Compliance officers end up chasing people, filling in guesses, and producing inventories that are immediately out of date.

No risk prioritisation means no starting point

Even when data is mapped, teams can't answer "where do we start?" Without a risk score on each data type — sensitivity, volume, third-party transfers — resource allocation is guesswork.

Consultants don't scale

A consultant-led data audit is expensive, slow, and produces a point-in-time snapshot. As your systems change, the inventory goes stale. There's no repeatable, scalable process built underneath.

The ZolaShield model

From unknown to audit-ready in four steps.

Every step is automated. Every output is structured. Your team completes a phone call — ZolaShield handles everything else.

01

Register your organisation and add the team members who handle personal data across departments. Takes under 10 minutes. No technical setup required.

02
