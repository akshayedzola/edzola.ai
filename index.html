How we work · ImpactOS

Programme first. Software last.

Every project begins with how the work actually runs. The tools come after the decisions, responsibilities and evidence are clear.

Explore the full method →
  1. 01
    Understand the work

    Follow the person, the money and the evidence through today’s process.

  2. 02
    Design it with you

    Agree on records, roles, decisions and where AI should stop.

  3. 03
    Build, move data, train

    Release useful parts early. Move old data carefully. Train in context.

  4. 04
    Run and improve

    Watch adoption and quality. Keep the system growing with the programme.
