How we work · ImpactOS

Programme first. Software last.

Scoping starts on a wall of sticky notes with the people who do the work, before anyone opens a laptop. If a spreadsheet is enough, we’ll say so.

  1. 01
    Listen

    We learn how your work really runs and match you with a practitioner who has built it before.

  2. 02
    Scope

    Clear requirements, an honest estimate and what we won’t do.

    ✓ Scope agreed
  3. 03
    Deliver

    Design with you, then build, move your data across and train in usable stages.

    ✓ Design locked✓ Tested
  4. 04
    Close

    Handover, documentation and an adoption check. We stay until it’s in use.

    ✓ In use
  5. 05
    Learn

    A retrospective. What worked goes back into ImpactOS for the next organisation.

  • Your data stays yoursWhatever the platform, you own it.
  • Humans decideAI handles volume. People handle consequence.
  • One named ownerYou always know who to call.
  • Done means adoptedNot the day it launches.
