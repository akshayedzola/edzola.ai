FundZola doesn't replace Zoho Books. It makes it actually usable for nonprofits.

Zoho Books is a powerful financial ledger — but it wasn't built for Indian NGO compliance workflows. FundZola connects to your existing Books account and adds the layer your finance team has been missing: AI-assisted data entry, 80G receipt generation, FCRA tagging, fund utilisation reporting, and a conversational interface to your financial data.

How it works

Setup in a day. Results from day one.

Capabilities

Six modules. Every finance workflow covered.

FundZola covers the full lifecycle of nonprofit finance — from a donor paying on Ketto to a fund utilisation report going to the board.

⬆️
M1 · DonorIngest
Bulk Donation Import
Upload donation CSVs from any platform — Ketto, Milaap, Razorpay, internal sheets. AI normalises, deduplicates, validates PAN, and pushes clean records to Zoho Books as Customer Payments.
  • Reads Ketto, Milaap, Razorpay, BillDesk export formats
  • Auto-detects FCRA vs Local donations
  • Flags missing PANs, name mismatches before push
  • Creates Zoho Books Contacts for new donors
🧾
M2 · ReceiptEngine
80G Receipt Generation
Generates legally compliant 80G receipts in bulk, with serial numbers, your NGO letterhead, and donor names exactly as per PAN records. Emails them automatically, tracks delivery.
  • Auto-serialised receipt numbers (no duplicates, ever)
  • Donor name pulled from PAN — no legal risk
  • Bulk email delivery with delivery status
  • Amendment workflow for receipt corrections (RAN)
🏷️
M3 · ExpenseMapper
AI Expense Tagging
Import expense bills and let AI suggest which grant and programme head each expense belongs to — based on your Grant Allocation Matrix. You confirm, it pushes to Zoho Books.
  • AI maps each expense to the right grant automatically
  • Flags admin overhead breaches per funder rules
  • Cost centre, function, and reporting tag auto-fill
  • Human approval required before every Books write
✦
M4 · Copilot
Finance Copilot (Chat)
Ask your financial data plain questions. "How much have we spent from the APF grant this quarter?" "Which programme heads are over budget?" Copilot pulls from Zoho Books live data.
  • Natural language queries on all financial data
  • Alerts: grant ceilings approaching, FCRA limits, etc.
  • Actionable outputs — not just answers
  • No training needed — works like a chat
📊
M5 · Fund Utilisation
Donor Reports, Auto-generated
Generate fund utilisation reports for each donor or grant in minutes. Pulls actuals from Zoho Books, compares against approved budgets, exports board-ready PDFs.
  • Actual vs. approved budget comparison per grant
  • Programme-head-wise expense breakdown
  • Admin overhead % flagged per funder ceiling
  • PDF export ready for board and donor submission
🔁
M6 · Reconciliation
Gateway Reconciliation
Match Razorpay and BillDesk settlement reports against donor payments in Zoho Books. Net out gateway fees and GST automatically. Human-approved before any record is updated.
  • Razorpay and BillDesk settlement file parsing
  • Auto-calculates gateway commission and GST
  • Posts net settlement amounts back to Zoho Books
  • Full reconciliation audit trail maintained

Indian NGO Compliance

80G. FCRA. CSR. All handled.

FundZola was built specifically for Indian nonprofit compliance. Every workflow reflects how regulators actually work — not a generic accounting tool retrofitted.

80G
Section 80G Receipts
Generate legally compliant 80G donation receipts in bulk with correct serial numbers, donor PAN details, and your NGO's registration number.
  • Auto-serialised receipt numbers (unique per FY)
  • Donor name exactly as per PAN — legally required
  • Receipt Amendment Notice (RAN) workflow
  • PDF with your NGO letterhead and stamp
  • Email delivery with delivery tracking
FCRA
FCRA Fund Segregation
FundZola automatically identifies and tags foreign contributions, maintains strict Local / FCRA fund separation, and never co-mingles accounts.
  • Auto-detect FCRA vs Local at donation upload
  • Separate COA entries for FCRA Income in Zoho Books
  • FCRA_UNCERTAIN cases always escalated to human
  • Audit log of every FCRA classification decision
  • FCRA utilisation tracking against permitted heads
CSR
CSR Grant Management
Manage corporate CSR grants with programme-level tracking, utilisation reporting against CSR-1 commitments, and donor-ready impact reports.
  • Grant Allocation Matrix maps CSR to programme heads
  • Budget vs actuals per CSR grant in real time
  • Admin overhead % tracked against funder ceiling
  • Fund Utilisation Report exported as PDF
  • Separate project tracking per CSR donor in Books
