The definition

One sentence to remember.

Is ImpactOS a product, a process or a platform? It depends on when you look at it.

ImpactOS
How EdZola works: one method, a growing body of knowledge, and the AI modules that run on them.

Services and products sit on top. ImpactOS is what they share, so every project makes the next one faster and every product starts from real delivery.

Today

A process

Written steps, owners and gates. It works with a spreadsheet, a template and a call.

Becoming

A platform

The same steps, with knowledge and AI modules doing the heavy lifting. A person still owns the outcome.

Produces

Products

When a module runs well without us, it gets its own name. DozAI, zolabs.ai and ZolaShield all come out of ImpactOS.

Think Toyota Production System. Nobody buys it, and every Toyota is made with it.

The layers

Three layers inside. Two things built on top.

Each layer changes at a different speed. That's the point. The method stays steady so knowledge and modules can move fast.

The method

The spine every engagement follows.

From an identified need to a solution the client owns, and back again with what we learned.

01Entry

Enquiry matched to an organisation, tagged to a service, routed to an owner.

02Convert

Clear requirements, estimate, accepted proposal. Won, lost or deferred recorded.

03Deliver

The service playbook, followed to adoption.

04Close

Acceptance, documentation, handover, retrospective.

05Learn

Learning Circle sends improvements back to the front.

Named owners

Every deal, project and module has one accountable person.

Human checkpoints

AI drafts. People review, decide and send.

One record per org

Everything we learn about an organisation lands in one place.
