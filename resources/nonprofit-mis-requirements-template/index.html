06 · Common questions

Practical questions teams ask.

Use these answers to clarify scope, compare approaches and identify the decisions your team needs to make before implementation.

How should a nonprofit use the nonprofit MIS requirements template?

A useful nonprofit MIS requirements template captures the programme journey, central record, roles, decisions, forms, indicators, permissions, integrations, migration and adoption measures. It helps teams compare proposals against operating reality instead of generic feature lists.

What fields or sections should the nonprofit MIS requirements template contain?

Start with requirements decision pack. The minimum useful record set covers programme model and scope, roles, decisions and permissions, records, fields and relationships, workflows, exceptions and approvals, reports, indicators and evidence and migration, integration, adoption and ownership. Remove any field that does not support a service, safeguard, decision or reporting obligation.

Who should complete and maintain the nonprofit MIS requirements template?

The team that owns requirements decision pack should maintain the resource, with input from frontline, programme, MEL or data, technology and leadership roles as relevant. Keep one named owner responsible for version control, review decisions and updates.

How can the nonprofit MIS requirements template be adapted for different programme sizes?

Keep the definition of requirements decision pack stable at every scale. A small programme can begin with one workflow and a few roles; a multi-state programme also needs delegated ownership, stronger permissions, shared definitions, integration rules and location-level quality review.

How can a team check that the nonprofit MIS requirements template is complete and useful?

Keep the source, definition, time, owner, status and review decision together. Monitor requirements with named owners, critical exceptions represented, proposals compared consistently and scope changes recorded, and preserve limitations instead of cleaning them out of the report.
