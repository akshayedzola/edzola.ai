Give every worker a safe caseload and every referral an owner.

06 · Question bank

Questions this guide answers.

These questions are part of EdZola’s mapped 1,500-question research bank. The answers below are visible, self-contained and reflected in the page’s structured data.

How should a nonprofit implement community health worker case management systems?

Use a phased sequence: map worker decisions and clinical boundaries, define minimum patient identity, build visit and referral workflow, test privacy on shared devices, connect facility confirmation where possible and review overdue and unresolved cases. Each stage needs a named owner and a review gate before the next stage begins.

What records and data fields are needed for community health worker case management systems?

Start with patient or household care episode. The minimum useful record set covers person, household and safe contact, worker assignment and visit, screening result and protocol version, referral, facility and due date, service received and evidence and follow-up, closure and escalation. Remove any field that does not support a service, safeguard, decision or reporting obligation.

Which staff roles and review steps should community health worker case management systems include?

Use a phased sequence: map worker decisions and clinical boundaries, define minimum patient identity, build visit and referral workflow, test privacy on shared devices, connect facility confirmation where possible and review overdue and unresolved cases. Each stage needs a named owner and a review gate before the next stage begins.

What are the most common mistakes when setting up community health worker case management systems?

The main risks are workers seeing unassigned patients, alerts without an escalation service, clinical interpretation embedded in code and unsafe contact after a sensitive screening. Test these with representative cases and keep the consequential judgement with a named person.

How should community health worker case management systems change for a small NGO versus a multi-state programme?

The answer depends on scale, roles, migration, integrations and internal ownership. Start with one complete workflow, make the assumptions explicit and expand only after the organisation can maintain the first release.
