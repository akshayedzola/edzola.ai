What ImpactOS is

A common spine with vertical-specific playbooks.

ImpactOS takes an organisation from an identified need to a delivered and adopted solution—then turns project learning into a better next engagement.

01 / COMMON

One operating spine

Entry, requirements, proposal, conversion, delivery, adoption and closure.

02 / OWNED

Vertical playbooks

CRM, data systems, HRMS, Zolabs, Zola Shield and custom AI each follow their own route.

03 / LEARNING

A loop, not a line

Retrospectives improve templates, tools, estimates and methods at every stage.

Before the proposal

Choose the lightest path to clear requirements.

The deal type and complexity determine the mix. Most proposals will use one or more of these routes.

Share templates

Use structured spreadsheets or templated processes when the customer can describe the requirement independently.

Share cloud skills

Guide the customer to prepare requirements, documents or structured inputs.

Clarify on a call

Work through ambiguity together when the deal is complex or the customer needs support.

Template + skill + call, in any combination → high-level requirements → estimate → proposal

Flag for a separate solution-routing framework

Multi-service or unclear deals need an internal decision: CRM or Creator? CRM or an EdZola-built solution? Creator or an EdZola-built solution? Name the primary service, supporting services, platform and accountable owner before proposing.

Vertical playbooks

Two active lanes for every service.

Convert turns an active opportunity into an accepted proposal. Deliver takes the converted deal to successful completion.

CRM

Strengthen relationships with donors, volunteers, partners, beneficiaries and other stakeholders.

Owner to refine
01 · Convert the deal
  1. Map relationship types
  2. Scope modules, pipelines, stages, workflows, reports and integrations
  3. Estimate and propose
  4. Record won, lost or deferred
02 · Complete delivery
  1. Run the CRM design workshop
  2. Validate fields, layouts and processes
  3. Connect securely; configure, migrate and integrate
  4. Test, train and hand over
  5. Monitor adoption and close

Data systems

Programme and operational systems for admissions, delivery, outcomes, placements and more.

Owner to refine
01 · Convert the deal
  1. Identify modules and user groups
  2. Scope functions, indicators, workflows, reports and integrations
  3. Estimate and propose
  4. Record won, lost or deferred
02 · Complete delivery
  1. Run the process and data workshop
  2. Confirm the data model, journeys and functional design
  3. Build, migrate and integrate
  4. Test, train and deploy
  5. Adapt, monitor and close

HRMS

Zoho People core HR, extended with the modules each organisation needs.

Owner to refine
01 · Convert the deal
  1. Capture the workforce, policies and required modules
  2. Select Zoho People plus Expense, Payroll and/or Recruit as needed
  3. Clarify complexity, estimate and propose
  4. Record won, lost or deferred
02 · Complete delivery
  1. Validate HR processes and approve the specification
  2. Configure Zoho People and selected modules
  3. Migrate data and test
  4. Train the internal POC and staff
  5. Deploy, monitor adoption and close

Zolabs

Voice AI research and evaluation studies—from a controlled pilot to a final report.

Owner to refine
01 · Convert the deal
  1. Confirm a Voice AI research or evaluation study
  2. Define participant count, language and complexity
  3. Share the estimate and study plan
  4. Confirm the engagement
02 · Complete delivery
  1. Configure and run the pilot
  2. Review feedback and make changes
  3. Run the full study
  4. Analyse and share the final report
  5. Accept and close

Custom AI solutions

Purpose-built AI workflows with explicit feasibility, safety and human-oversight checks.

Owner to refine

Common closing process

Close the project. Keep the learning moving.

Completion means the customer can use and own the solution—not simply that the technical work has stopped.

01Customer acceptance
02Documentation & training

04Retrospective
05Learning Circle
GATE 01

Deal converted

Review source, requirements, objections, estimation and promises handed to delivery.

GATE 02

Design locked

Review wrong assumptions, missing questions, scope shifts and reusable patterns.

GATE 03

Delivery complete

Review estimate versus actual, rework, quality, migration and technical learning.

GATE 04

Adoption reviewed

Review real use, training gaps, outcomes and follow-up needs.
