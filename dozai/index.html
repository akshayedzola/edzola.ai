The DozAI system

Four ways to put DozAI to work.

Start with a mission: one workflow, done start to finish. Add a teammate for an ongoing role, package your expertise as a skill, or work with us to decide where AI belongs at all.

DozAI Missions

Hand over a whole workflow, start to finish.

A mission is one outcome with clear rules: verify where 2,000 graduates are working, chase every missing document, collect every grantee’s milestone evidence. DozAI runs each case until it is done or needs a person.

Human judgement by design

AI handles volume. People handle consequence.

Every DozAI mission starts as an apprentice. It works under review, learns from your team’s corrections, and earns more autonomy only after it passes. People keep the decisions that affect someone’s rights, money or opportunity.

ConfigureSet methods, rules, examples, permissions, and quality standards.
CalibrateReview early cases and correct what it gets wrong.
ExecuteLet DozAI complete repeatable work across the workflow.
EscalateRoute uncertainty, high-risk cases, and decisions to people.
AuditSample outputs, evaluate quality, and improve the capability.

AI should not make expertise less valuable. It should make the best expertise available to more people.
