Introducing DozAI by EdZola

AI that gets nonprofit work done.

Your team doesn’t need another AI tool to operate. Hand DozAI a whole workflow, the follow-ups, checks and paperwork you repeat every cycle. It does the volume, shows its evidence, and brings the decisions that matter back to your people.

A different starting point

Start with the work. Not the prompt.

Another AI tool

Your team learns prompts, moves data between apps, checks every response, and still runs the whole process.

DozAI

You set the outcome, the rules, what counts as evidence, and where a person must decide. DozAI does the repeatable work in between and hands back what needs a person, with the evidence attached.

The DozAI system

Four ways to put DozAI to work.

Start with a mission: one workflow, done start to finish. Add a teammate for an ongoing role, package your expertise as a skill, or work with us to decide where AI belongs at all.

DozAI Missions

Hand over a whole workflow, start to finish.

A mission is one outcome with clear rules: verify where 2,000 graduates are working, chase every missing document, collect every grantee’s milestone evidence. DozAI runs each case until it is done or needs a person.

Placement & retention · pilot open · Document completeness · Grantee milestone evidence
DozAI Workforce

Add an AI teammate to a recurring role.

A specialised teammate that learns how your team works, works across your tools, remembers context between tasks, and knows when to ask for help.

MEL associate · Programme analyst · Fundraising associate
DozAI Skills

Turn expertise into a reusable capability.

A skill packages a proven method with its rubrics, tools, limits and rules for when to ask a person, so the same quality of work happens every time. Expertise that runs, not a prompt template.

Theory of Change · Survey design · Outcome analysis
Voice + Advisory

Reach people, then design the system around them.

Use AI phone calls in local languages to reach people at the last mile, with EdZola’s team helping assess readiness, shape workflows, and put the right safeguards in place.

Voice follow-up · Readiness assessment · Workflow design
Missions for social impact

One platform. Missions shaped for each sector.

Every mission uses the same building blocks: outreach, voice, documents, rules, review and audit. We pick work with high volume, clear evidence and an obvious point where a person decides.

Human judgement by design

AI handles volume. People handle consequence.

Every DozAI mission starts as an apprentice. It works under review, learns from your team’s corrections, and earns more autonomy only after it passes. People keep the decisions that affect someone’s rights, money or opportunity. See how escalation and override should work →

Set upSet methods, rules, examples, permissions, and quality standards.
CorrectReview early cases and correct what it gets wrong.
RunLet DozAI complete repeatable work across the workflow.
Hand overPass unclear cases, high-risk cases, and decisions to people.
AuditSpot-check results, rate quality, and improve the skill.
