06 · Common questions

Practical questions teams ask.

Use these answers to clarify scope, compare approaches and identify the decisions your team needs to make before implementation.

Can AI safely help a nonprofit with MEL data quality assistant?

A MEL data-quality assistant can apply documented checks, compare related records and prepare a queue of missing, inconsistent or unusual cases with source links. It should never silently change source data or invent values to make a dataset complete.

What information does an AI mission for MEL data quality assistant need?

Start with data-quality exception. The minimum useful record set covers dataset and approved version, record and source link, rule, threshold and rationale, exception and possible explanation, assigned owner and correction and review outcome and change history. Remove any field that does not support a service, safeguard, decision or reporting obligation.

What can AI execute and what must a person decide in MEL data quality assistant?

People should retain responsibility for correcting source records, accepting a valid outlier, changing an indicator rule and deciding dataset fitness for reporting. Software or AI may prepare evidence and queues, but it should not silently make those decisions.

What evidence and audit trail should MEL data quality assistant produce?

Keep the source, definition, time, owner, status and review decision together. Monitor exceptions confirmed, time to resolution, false-positive rate and repeated error pattern reduced, and preserve limitations instead of cleaning them out of the report.

Where can an AI mission for MEL data quality assistant fail or require escalation?

The main risks are anomaly treated as an error, missing values imputed into source, rules changing without versioning and teams overwhelmed by low-value alerts. Test these with representative cases and keep the consequential judgement with a named person.
