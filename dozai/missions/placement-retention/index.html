Sound familiar?

Placement is the easy number. Retention is where it falls apart.

You can tell a funder how many people you trained and placed. Ask what happened six months later and it becomes a scramble.

01

The report is due. The number is a guess.

Retention gets estimated from whoever picked up the phone, not from the full cohort.

02

Half the phone numbers have changed.

Graduates switch SIMs, cities and jobs. One missed call and they drop off the list.

03

Calls happen between batches.

Trainers and placement officers squeeze in follow-ups, ask different questions and log answers differently.

04

Nobody can show the evidence.

When a funder or auditor asks “how do you know?”, the answer lives in a spreadsheet and someone’s memory.

The people who most need a follow-up, the ones who lost the job, are the ones you’re least likely to reach.

How the mission runs

You set the rules once. DozAI runs every checkpoint.

The mission works through your cohort at 30, 90 and 180 days (or whatever schedule your funder uses), and keeps going until every case reaches a clear state.

Load the cohort

From your MIS, CRM or a spreadsheet. Consent and contact rules travel with each record.

Reach out

WhatsApp first, then a voice call in the graduate’s language. Retries within the contact windows you allow.

Ask what matters

Your questions, with follow-ups that branch: employer, role, start date, income band, reasons for leaving.

Check against records

Spots when answers contradict the placement on file, or when something sounds off.

Propose an outcome

Classifies each case and attaches the evidence. Clear cases are ready to approve.

Your team decides

Reviewers see only conflicts, sensitive answers and unclear cases. Approved results write back to your system.

A case packet, not a call log

Each graduate’s result comes with everything a funder or auditor would ask for.

  • Call recording and transcript, or the WhatsApp thread
  • Structured answers: employer, role, start date, income band
  • What changed since the last checkpoint, and why
  • Who reviewed it, what they decided, and when
  • A cohort view: retention by checkpoint, centre, trade and employer

And one thing most follow-up efforts never capture: the graduates who need help again. Anyone looking for work can be routed back to your placement team.

Tools, automation and human judgement

AI handles the volume. People handle the consequence.

We’re explicit about who does what. Nothing that affects a graduate’s record or your reported outcomes is decided without a person able to see why.

Automated

Repeatable, reversible work

  • Scheduling checkpoints and retries
  • Sending reminders and switching channels
  • Running the interview in the graduate’s language
  • Transcribing and pulling out the answers
  • Honouring opt-outs immediately
AI-assisted

Drafts a person can check

  • Proposing an outcome for each case
  • Flagging contradictions with your records
  • Summarising why a graduate left a job
  • Drafting the cohort retention report
Stays human

Decisions with consequence

  • Whether conflicting evidence counts as an outcome
  • What meets your funder’s definition of “placed”
  • Anything involving distress, safety or suspected fraud
  • Changing the definitions themselves

What DozAI is

One mission. Built on a platform for many.

DozAI isn’t a placement-tracking app. It’s a platform for running missions: outcome-focused workflows where AI does the repeatable work and people keep the decisions that carry consequence.

Placement and retention is the first. Every mission after it reuses the same foundation for cases, evidence, follow-up, review and audit, so your second mission doesn’t need a second system.

How we choose a mission, and what’s on the list.

AI agents do well on work with bounded judgement: a known goal, a finite set of evidence, clear rules, and an obvious point where they must stop and ask a person. We test every candidate against the same criteria.

What we won’t automate

Award decisions for grants, scholarships or benefits. Safeguarding triage. Hiring and rejection. The volume is tempting, but the consequence for a person is too high for an AI to own.

Questions we’re asked

Before you apply

Does this replace our placement team?

No. It takes the repetitive calling off their plate so they can spend time on the graduates who need help: the ones who lost a job, or are struggling in one. Your team still owns every decision that goes into a report.

What about consent and our graduates’ data?

We agree the consent basis, what data the mission can see, and how long recordings are kept before anything runs. Graduates can opt out at any point and that’s honoured immediately. Your data stays within your organisation’s own tenant, and a lesson learned from your cases never moves to another organisation’s mission. Once the mission is complete, we can delete your data on request, including every call recording, transcript and message. We design this in line with India’s DPDP Act.

Which languages does it work in?

Voice calls run on Zolabs, EdZola’s multilingual voice platform, built for conversations across Indian languages. We’ll confirm the languages your cohort needs during the mission contract week.

What if a graduate doesn’t pick up?

DozAI retries within the windows you set, switches between WhatsApp and voice, and stops at the limit you agree. “Unreachable” is a recorded outcome with every attempt logged, not a blank row.

Which systems does it work with?

For the pilot, a spreadsheet export is enough. If you run on Zoho Creator, a CRM or another MIS, we can read from and write back to it directly.

What does the pilot cost?

We’ll talk about it on our first call. The cohort is small on purpose and we want the right partners in it, not the ones who can pay most.
