Placement & Retention is our first mission. Think of it as a verification assistant for your placement team: it follows up with graduates, collects their answers, checks them against your records, and brings anything that needs judgement back to your people.

Today we design each mission together with our pilot partners. Over time, organisations will be able to set up and run more missions of their own on DozAI.

Sound familiar?

Placement is the easy number. Retention is where it falls apart.

You can tell a funder how many people you trained and placed. Ask what happened six months later and it becomes a scramble.

01

The report is due. The number is a guess.

Retention gets estimated from whoever picked up the phone, not the whole cohort.

02

Half the phone numbers have changed.

Graduates switch SIMs, cities and jobs. One missed call and they drop off the list.

03

Calls happen between batches.

Trainers squeeze in follow-ups, ask different questions and log answers differently.

04

The people who need help go unseen.

Graduates who lost their job are the least likely to pick up, and the most likely to need you.

How this mission works

Four steps. Your team stays in charge.

Share your graduate data

Upload a spreadsheet of your graduates. Together we agree when to follow up, what to ask, and what your funders need to see.

DozAI follows up

Graduates get WhatsApp messages and AI voice calls in their language, with retries when they don’t answer.

Answers are recorded

Job status, employer, role, salary band and any changes are captured the same way for every graduate.

Your team reviews and acts

You get an organised report, a short list of unclear cases to check, and a list of graduates who need help.

What your team gets

A report you can take to your monthly review, and to your funder.

Every follow-up round ends with a cohort view, a complete record for each graduate, and a clear list of who needs your team.

Trust & data protection

Your graduates’ data, handled with care.

Consent and AI disclosure

Graduates are told the message or call comes from your organisation and uses an AI assistant. They can ask for a person, or opt out at any time.

Who sees responses

Only the people your organisation names, and the EdZola team running your pilot.

Recordings and transcripts

Kept so your team can check any answer. Deleted on request, including once the mission is complete.

Where your data lives

Within your organisation’s own secure space, never shared with another organisation’s mission. We design this in line with India’s DPDP Act.

When a person decides

Conflicting answers, anything involving distress or safety, and any change to a reported outcome.

Deletion on request

Ask us to delete your graduates’ data, messages and recordings after the pilot, and we will.

Does this replace our placement team?

No. It takes the repetitive calling off their plate so they can spend time on the graduates who need help. Your team still owns every decision that goes into a report.

Which languages does it work in?

Voice calls run on Zolabs, EdZola’s multilingual voice platform for conversations across Indian languages. We confirm the languages your graduates need in week one.

What if a graduate doesn’t pick up?

We retry within the limits you set, switching between WhatsApp and voice. Anyone we can’t reach is reported as unknown, with every attempt logged, not as employed or unemployed.

Is this just an AI calling bot?

No. Calling is one part. The assistant keeps a consistent record for each graduate, cross-checks answers with your records, separates self-reported from verified outcomes, and flags who needs help. Your team reviews what matters.
